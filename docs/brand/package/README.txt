06-Guidelines — Visual usage guide.

USE
ERP header: horizontal. Collapsed sidebar: mark. Login/splash: stacked. Marketing and large documents: full-detail. Dark surfaces: dark or white treatment. One-colour printing: navy or white.

CLEAR SPACE & SIZE
Keep at least 10% of logo height clear around the full/stacked/wordmark. For horizontal, keep 25% of its height clear. Keep 10% of mark width clear around standalone mark.
Suggested minimum display widths: full-detail 480px; stacked 240px; horizontal 220px; wordmark 180px; mark 32px. Favicon sizes 16/32px are included, but fold and stack detail is naturally reduced at that scale.

PRESERVATION
The approved master geometry remains unchanged. Horizontal layout repositions and scales the approved mark and lettering. Dark treatment lifts gradient brightness and uses light text; monochrome intentionally removes shading. Never stretch, redraw, rotate or substitute a typeface. Retain aspect ratio. Use full-detail only where its tagline remains readable. These are RGB digital assets; for colour-critical commercial printing, ask the printer to proof a CMYK conversion.

COLOURS
Sapphire #21407A is the UI primary from the supplied branding. Logo ink #081B30, blue #00539A and cyan #12B6D9 are supporting colours used in the approved artwork. Tokens are palette values, not a guarantee of accessibility for every colour pairing.
