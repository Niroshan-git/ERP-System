## Overview

A Job Card tracks the execution of a specific operation within a Work Order. It represents one step in the production routing.

## Business Purpose

Breaks a Work Order down into individual operations (e.g., cutting, welding, assembly) that can be tracked, timed, and assigned to specific workstations — the foundation for shop-floor execution tracking and eventual OEE calculation.

## Where to Find It

Manufacturing → Job Cards (`/manufacturing/job-cards`). Job Cards are auto-generated by ERPNext when a Work Order with operations is submitted.

## Prerequisites

```config
required: Work Order (submitted, with operations defined in the BOM)
```

## Document Lifecycle

```flow
Open
Work In Progress
Completed
```

Job Cards also branch to **Cancelled**. Currently, only read-only viewing and Cancel are available in the Ceylon Stack frontend.

## Available Actions

View (read-only) · Cancel

```callout:warn
Job Card execution actions (Start, Pause, Complete, time logging) are PLANNED but not yet built. This is tracked as MFG-JOBCARD-2.
```

## Stock Impact

NO DIRECT STOCK IMPACT from Job Cards. Stock movement happens at the Stock Entry level (Material Transfer and Manufacture completion).

## Accounting Impact

NO DIRECT ACCOUNTING IMPACT from Job Cards.

## Related Documents

```flow
Work Order
Job Card
```

Job Cards are children of a Work Order — each operation in the Work Order's routing generates a Job Card.

## Limitations

The Ceylon Stack frontend currently provides read-only access to Job Cards with Cancel capability only. Time logging, operation execution (Start/Pause/Complete), and workstation assignment actions are planned for a future package (`MFG-JOBCARD-2`). The MES/OEE calculation service is a separate, not-yet-started FastAPI service.
